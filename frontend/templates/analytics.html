💡 Big Data Analytical Note: Correlation vs. Causation in Logistics

Statistical correlation indicates observable co-occurrence (e.g., shipments during monsoon months or heavy truck transit routes exhibit higher delay frequencies). However, correlation does not guarantee direct causation without controlled attribution (e.g., heavy trucks may predominantly be scheduled on long-distance interstate highways with roadwork, rather than the vehicle itself causing the delay). Predictive intelligence accounts for combined multi-factor influences.
